CShapes 2.0 — attribution and licence notice
=============================================

This folder contains a SIMPLIFIED derivative of the CShapes 2.0 dataset
(file: cshapes-2.0-gw.simplified.topojson).

Dataset
  Schvitz, G., Girardin, L., Rüegger, S., Weidmann, N. B., Cederman, L.-E., &
  Gleditsch, K. S. (2022). Mapping the International System, 1886–2019: The
  CShapes 2.0 Dataset. Journal of Conflict Resolution, 66(1), 144–161.
  https://doi.org/10.1177/00220027211013563
  Data record: https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000487987
  Project page: https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/

Source of this copy
  inst/extdata/cshapes_2_gw.topojson.xz from the CRAN package "cshapes" 2.0
  (Weidmann, Schvitz & Girardin, 2021), via the GitHub mirror
  https://github.com/cran/cshapes. State-system membership follows
  Gleditsch & Ward (GW). Coverage: 1886-01-01 to 2019-12-31.

Changes made (see scripts/build-history-data.mjs)
  * Geometry generalised with Douglas-Peucker, 3 km tolerance, topology preserved
    (shared borders stay shared). ~97% of rings kept; features smaller than about
    3 km may be missing. A handful of microscopic (<70 km2) bow-ties may remain.
  * Attributes reduced to those the app uses; area (km2) added, computed from the
    ORIGINAL unsimplified geometry.
  * No boundary has been added, moved by hand, or invented.
